Now in our second century and 3rd generation of farming, Mikami Vineyards produces single vineyard, handcrafted wines from the premier blocks of the family vineyard estate. This wine is a tribute to my father who for over 40 years managed our 15-acre vineyard by hand, but never saw the release of these wines. Winery Bio

As sixth-generation farmers, we've learned that high-quality, handcrafted wine requires an immense amount of heart and hard work. Nothing can replicate the years we've sown into the land and our family's legacy. Since 1900, with focus and fortitude, we've learned that excellence takes endurance, distinction takes determination, and grace takes grit. That's why we've taken matters into our own hands. We pour our heart into every step of the process, from soil to shelf; we're committed to making the best possible beverage for our customers and the planet. Our 100% estate-grown wines are produced from sustainably farmed vineyards and meticulously made with no unnecessary additives and an unwavering commitment to quality. The result is a classic collection of naturally smooth, fruit-driven wines, perfect for enjoying in everyday life. With over a century on Henry Ranch in the heart of Lodi Wine Country, our philosophy has been and will always be: to honestly make wine that is beautifully balanced and consciously crafted with grace and grit.

Winery Bio

Every great wine has a story, and at Oak Farm Vineyards, that story begins in the heart of Lodi, California, where history, family, and a passion for exceptional winemaking come together. The estate's roots date back to the 1800s, when William DeVries established a beautiful agricultural property surrounded by majestic oak trees. Today, those same historic grounds provide the setting for one of Lodi's most distinctive family-owned wineries. Under the ownership of the Panella family, Oak Farm Vineyards has evolved into a premium wine destination while remaining true to its agricultural heritage. With generations of farming experience, the family understands that exceptional wine begins in the vineyard. What makes Oak Farm truly special is its ability to combine Old World inspiration with New World innovation. From elegant Cabernet Sauvignon and vibrant Sauvignon Blanc to beautifully balanced Zinfandel, every wine is crafted to showcase its varietal character, sense of place, and outstanding quality. Sustainability is also central to the winery's philosophy. Through responsible vineyard practices and a commitment to the land, Oak Farm helps preserve Lodi's rich agricultural legacy for future generations.

Winery Bio

Anaya Vineyards is the story of a third-generation farming family rooted in Lodi, California. Our family vineyards in the Clements Hills AVA represent generations of hard work, agricultural heritage, and a deep connection to the land. Founded by winemaker Gerardo Espinosa, Anaya Vineyards brings that legacy to life through small-production, low-intervention wines that stay true to the vineyard, the varietal, and the vintage. From fresh, vibrant Carbonic Tempranillo to classic European-style Cabernet Sauvignon, our wines bridge tradition and innovation. Above all, we create authentic, food-friendly wines meant to bring people together.
